## What the book covers

Nine chapters and 36 figures, from the AI customer journey (four stages, ten steps) to how to measure results. Four reading paths: shop owners, marketers, researchers and beginners. It is not a book of tricks for getting AI to recommend a business.

## Limits of the data

Requests to the author's website grew from 53 on day one (2026-03-03) to 105.5 million cumulative by 2026-10-08, as recorded by Cloudflare (AI systems, search engines and automated bots). The Cloudflare dashboard is not public. The figures are platform scale and cannot be compared with a single shop's website. Requests before 2026-08-24 may include duplicates. 2.82 million of the requests were made by the site's own Cloudflare AI Search; the 11th revision still includes them, and they will be deducted in the next revision. A visit by AI to read content does not mean it has been used for training or indexed.
