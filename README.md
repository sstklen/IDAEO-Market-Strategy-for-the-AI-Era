## What the book says

**One definition.** Marketing is getting the right customers to find you, trust you and choose you. Media space and search rankings used to decide that; now AI answers do. So the marketing audience grows from humans to humans plus four AI audiences.

**One name.** IDAEO = ID → IDA → AEO. ID helps AI recognize who you are. IDA (Identity Data Authority) provides verifiable identity data from an official source, like an embassy in the AI world. AEO gets AI answers to use your content.

**One theory.** The Seven Laws are the core, the Five Dimensions the framework, the Nine Elements the components.

- **Five Dimensions:** Audience Strategy (who it is for), Journey Design (which steps to take), Trust and Verification (why anyone should believe it), Service Tiers (which tier to deliver), Performance Measurement (how results are counted).
- **Five Marketing Audiences** and six Optimization Domains.
- **The AI Customer Journey:** four phases, ten steps (S1–S10), each with a diagnostic question.
- **Three Service Tiers:** AEO Exposure (get read first), AEO+ Word of Mouth (get mentioned), IDA Brand PR (accurate answers, visits and returns).

**First-party data.** Requests to the author's website grew from 53 on day one (March 3, 2026) to 105.5 million cumulative by October 8, as recorded by Cloudflare: 220 days of field testing. Chapter 7 publishes the results of the author's own site and of the first batch of field-test merchants. The author's AEO checkers have tested more than 20,000 websites.

**A living theory.** Revised regularly, testing earlier judgments against new data.

Nine chapters and 36 figures, with reading paths for shop owners, marketers, researchers and beginners.

## Limits of the data

The 105.5 million requests include AI systems, search engines and automated bots. The Cloudflare dashboard is not public. The figures are platform scale and cannot be compared with a single shop's website. Requests before 2026-08-24 may include duplicates. 2.82 million of the requests were made by the site's own Cloudflare AI Search; the 11th revision still includes them, and they will be deducted in the next revision. A visit by AI to read content does not mean it has been used for training or indexed.
